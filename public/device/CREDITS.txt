Original three-dimensional device model
The geometry in components/mobile/phone3d/model.ts was created for this
portfolio. It is a nonofficial visualization of the Burgundy iPhone 18 Pro Max,
not an Apple model or manufacturing CAD file. Its main enclosure measures
77.98 x 163.43 x 8.75 mm, following Apple's accessory dimensional drawing,
dated September 9, 2026, sheet 1 (PDF page 2):
https://developer.apple.com/download/files/accessories/dimensional-drawings/iphone-18-pro-max.pdf
The drawing specifies a 72.86 x 158.31 mm display active area, inset 2.56 mm
from the housing, and 75.58 x 161.03 mm front cover glass. Detail A provides
these corner coordinate pairs, measured inward from the bounding-box corner:
(0,19.43), (0.04,13.90), (0.92,8.46), (3.80,3.80), (8.46,0.92),
(13.90,0.04), (19.43,0), all in millimeters.
The smooth curves between those points and their inward-offset contours are
interpolated approximations authored for this portfolio, not supplied CAD.
Apple's consumer specifications round the main dimensions to 78.0 x 163.4 mm:
https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/
Hardware details not dimensioned in that reference, optical interiors, and
material settings remain visual estimates based on product photographs.
The reference drawing is copyrighted by Apple and is not redistributed here.
